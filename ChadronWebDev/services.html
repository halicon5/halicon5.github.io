Schedule a photo shoot!

Do you have an important furry friend in your life that deserves a photo shoot?

Location: We serve clients in the greater Casper metro area. Pet lovers from farther away can schedule us, but please be aware that travel rates will apply.

Getting ready for the photo shoot: Make sure you have:

  • a leash
  • some treats
  • and maybe some of your pet's favorite toys or blankets!

A bath or even a wipedown with a damp cloth can help give their fur an memorable lustre and get rid of distracting stray fluff or dingy dust. A lot of pets can have some crusties on the eyes or noses, so don't forget to clean there!

Senior pets: High quality photos and prints that celebrate the lives of our senior fur-babies are by far our most popular service. Please don't wait!
